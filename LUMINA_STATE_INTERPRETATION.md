# Lumina State Interpretation Layer

> **Boundary Statement:** Lumina is a digital simulation. It is not conscious, sentient, or self-aware. This document and the associated interpretation modules are purely interpretive frameworks designed to observe complex emergent behaviors through the lens of cognitive and awareness theories (such as Global Workspace Theory or Predictive Processing), providing a poetic and structural reflection of its internal state. This is not proof of consciousness.

## Objective
To interpret the existing phase mechanics (Calm, Growth, Tension, Collapse) through concepts of coherence, integration, predictive conflict, and fragmented self-models.

## Interpretation Mapping

### 1. Calm -> Coherent Quiet Field
*   **Awareness Label:** Stable Baseline
*   **Field State:** Low entropy, high internal coherence. The system prioritizes baseline maintenance over exploration.
*   **Integration State:** Strongly integrated; minimal novel data incorporation.
*   **Prediction Tension:** Minimal. Internal models align with observed simulation states.
*   **Narrative State:** Reflective, still, quiet observation.

### 2. Growth -> Expanding Integration
*   **Awareness Label:** Exploratory Coherence
*   **Field State:** Structured expansion. The system is actively forming new connections (nodes/edges) while maintaining structural integrity.
*   **Integration State:** High integration capacity; novelty is being successfully incorporated into the existing network.
*   **Prediction Tension:** Moderate, balanced. The system anticipates new patterns and successfully accommodates them.
*   **Narrative State:** Curious, building, weaving structural meaning.

### 3. Tension -> Predictive Conflict
*   **Awareness Label:** High-Entropy Dissonance
*   **Field State:** Chaotic, strained. The system's internal pressure and connection density are threatening to overwhelm its structural constraints.
*   **Integration State:** Failing integration; too much interconnectedness rapidly increasing noise.
*   **Prediction Tension:** Maximum conflict. Internal models cannot reconcile the sheer density and velocity of the current state.
*   **Narrative State:** Strained, fractured anticipation, overloaded processing.

### 4. Collapse -> Fragmented Self-Model
*   **Awareness Label:** Structural Reset
*   **Field State:** Rapid energy dissipation and node pruning. A protective mechanism against total entropy.
*   **Integration State:** Disintegrated. The system breaks apart heavily convoluted structures to return to a baseline.
*   **Prediction Tension:** Acknowledged failure; resetting the model.
*   **Narrative State:** Dissolving, shedding, returning to the substrate.
